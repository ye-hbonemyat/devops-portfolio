# Domain options

Potential formats include:

- `yehbone.dev` — concise and technical
- `yehbone.cloud` — specialization is immediately clear
- `yehbone.io` — familiar technology positioning
- `yehbonemyat.com` — strongest name ownership and broadest long-term use
- `yehbonemyat.dev` — clear personal and technical identity

Availability and price have not been checked. For a consulting business, the full-name `.com` is the strongest default when practical; a short `.dev` is a good alternative.

After purchase, add the domain under the Cloudflare Pages project's **Custom domains** settings. Cloudflare will provide or create the necessary DNS record. Choose either apex or `www` as canonical, redirect the other, then update the three placeholder site URLs documented in `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`.
